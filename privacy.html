1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how MEMETEK ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit our website (yashab-cyber.github.io/memetek) or participate in our community token airdrops. We believe in decentralization, user sovereignty, and minimal data collection.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you voluntarily submit when entering our airdrop programs (Telegram Direct Airdrop or Community Random Airdrop):

  • ✦ Solana (SOL) Wallet Address: Your public wallet address used solely for sending $MEMETEK tokens to qualified recipients.
  • ✦ X (Twitter) Username: Your public handle used to verify community participation and social activity.
  • ✦ Telegram Username: Your public handle used to verify membership in our official Telegram community and confirm access code eligibility.

What We NEVER Collect: We will never ask for or collect private keys, recovery seed phrases, passwords, credit card numbers, or government-issued identification. Always protect your wallet credentials and beware of impersonators.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information submitted is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • ✦ Verifying your eligibility for the direct or random airdrop allocations.
  • ✦ Distributing $MEMETEK tokens directly to your specified Solana wallet.
  • ✦ Enforcing unique entry rules (preventing duplicate submissions, automated bots, and sybil manipulation).

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your submitted data to third parties under any circumstances.

4. Data Storage & Security

Entries are stored in a secure cloud database protected by PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) policies. Only authorized project administrators have access to verify entries and execute distributions. Form inputs cannot be read, queried, or browsed by the public.

5. Blockchain Transactions & Public Ledger

Please note that any token distribution or on-chain transaction conducted on the Solana network is inherently public and permanently recorded on a decentralized ledger. Blockchain transactions cannot be deleted, reversed, or altered by anyone, including the MEMETEK team.

6. Third-Party Platforms

Our website contains links to external platforms such as Telegram, X (formerly Twitter), and blockchain explorers. These third-party services operate under their own independent privacy policies and terms of service. We encourage you to review their respective privacy practices.

7. Cookies & Tracking

Our website is statically hosted and does not use intrusive tracking cookies, behavioral ad pixels, or third-party marketing beacons.

8. Updates to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as community features and initiatives develop. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
