FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS.

Clear answers to the most common questions regarding MEMETEK, token distribution, airdrop eligibility, and community safety.

MEMETEK is a decentralized, community-first meme coin and creative collective where viral humor meets blockchain innovation. Built on Solana, our mission is to provide an open, transparent, and gatekeeping-free space for crypto lovers, meme creators, and tech enthusiasts to collaborate, experiment, and have fun together.

We emphasize organic community culture over corporate agendas, proving that the strongest projects are powered by active, engaged communities.

We offer two distinct pathways to participate in the genesis airdrop:

  • Direct Telegram Airdrop (50 Spots): Reserved for the first 50 community members who join our official Telegram group, retrieve the secret access code, and submit their Solana wallet address on the Telegram Airdrop Page. This is a guaranteed 100% distribution.
  • Community Random Airdrop (1,000 Winners): Open to everyone. Fill out your details on the Community Airdrop Page, follow our official channels, and enter the verifiable random drawing.

MEMETEK is built natively on the Solana blockchain. We chose Solana for its 400ms finality, industry-leading throughput, and sub-cent transaction costs, making high-frequency meme coin trading accessible to everyone without punitive gas fees.

You can use any self-custodial Solana wallet including Phantom, Solflare, or Backpack. Always ensure you control your private keys.

The official contract address is marked as COMING SOON in our top contract banner. When deployed, the verified CA will be posted strictly on our official website (memetek.site) and pinned on our official Telegram announcement channel.

Crucial reminder: Never purchase tokens from unverified contracts sent in private DMs or posted by imitation social accounts.
